Starkeeper: defend a newborn star from the void swarm in a neon twin-stick arcade game. Pilot the last lighthouse ship, blast the moths of the void, gather their light, and choose upgrades between waves. Complete with a title screen, escalating waves, a boss, upgrade choices, pause, game over and restart, synthesized sound, and a HUD.
